Contributing

Full guide: wiki/contributing.

- Issues and PRs: use the available templates (blank issues are closed automatically by .github/ISSUE_TEMPLATE/config.yml).
- CI checks: go vet ./..., go test -count=1 ./..., make test-integration, cd web && bun test, make vet-svelte.
- License: MIT — by contributing you agree that the LICENSE applies to your contribution.
